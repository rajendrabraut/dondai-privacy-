Dondai Content Blocker Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Dondai Content Blocker for Chrome, version 0.5.0.

Data used to filter sites

Chrome evaluates request URLs against rules bundled with the extension. Dondai reads the current tab URL when you open the popup to show its hostname and manage site exceptions. A local content script checks the page for marked ad placeholders and hides matching elements when cleanup is enabled.

Storage and retention

Chrome stores your filtering settings, paused hostnames and custom blocked domains in your browser profile. Dondai retains those settings until you change or remove them or uninstall the extension. Chrome can display a per-tab rule action count on the extension icon. Dondai does not maintain browsing history, a request log or a header capture.

Sharing and transfers

Dondai performs filtering on your device. The extension does not transmit your browsing activity or settings to its developer or third parties and does not use analytics or telemetry. It does not sell user data.
